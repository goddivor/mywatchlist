Both shows have interesting settings which draw on 20th century aesthetics but in very different ways. Both follow young girls traveling through a flawed and broken world and making peace/being happy despite the obvious flaws. Both and slow paced and atmospheric, though Kino's Journey has more action and violence and is much darker. Neither show is particularly narrative-driven, though Kino's journey is the more episodic of the two. They both examine what it means to be human. Girls' Last Tour is a lot more calming than Kino's Journey though both can be depressing at times. Ultimately both shows have uplifting messages about life.
